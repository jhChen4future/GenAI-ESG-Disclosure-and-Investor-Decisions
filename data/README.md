# Data availability

The participant-level research data and final statistical-analysis scripts are not currently included in this repository because they have not yet been recovered from the original computer.

No synthetic replacement is presented as the original study data. If data or analysis materials are added later, the release will be subject to privacy, research-ethics, and de-identification review and will include an appropriate data dictionary and access statement.

## Interpretation warning

Please do not directly reuse the thesis conclusions as established findings. The study sample only met the minimum planned statistical-power requirement. Any aggregate estimates reported in the thesis are best treated as exploratory patterns with possible predictive or correlational value. Researchers interested in the topic should independently replicate the design with a suitably planned sample and analysis protocol.

For an urgent research request, contact the author through the associated academic homepage.
